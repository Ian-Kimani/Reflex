# Reflex: Demo Script

**Total Time:** ~4 minutes
**Goal:** Demonstrate the end-to-end flow clearly, highlighting the pain points solved for each persona and showcasing the new Gamification Engine.

## 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
*(Presenter 1)*
"Hello panel. Today we are presenting Reflex. Small retailers in Kenya are losing money and customer trust because they coordinate deliveries over chaotic WhatsApp chats. Let's see how Reflex fixes this by providing a premium, single source of truth."

## 2. The Retailer View (1 minute)
*(Presenter 1 switches to the Retailer Screen)*
"I am a Retailer. I just received a phone order for a blender. 
Instead of texting a group chat and hoping someone replies, I log the request here in our beautifully designed Reflex dashboard."
- **Action:** Fill out the Delivery Request Form (Customer Name: John Doe, Phone: 0712345678, Address: Westlands, Item: Blender).
- **Action:** Click Dispatch Delivery.
"My request immediately appears in my 'Active Requests' list, with the status 'Open'. Now, I have a permanent, trackable record of this delivery."

## 3. The Dispatcher View (1 minute)
*(Presenter 2 takes over, switches to Dispatcher Dashboard)*
"I am the Dispatcher. This is my command center. I can see all open requests across the city visually laid out on my Kanban board."
- **Action:** Point out the new 'Blender' request from the Retailer.
"I see a request for Westlands. I know my rider, Sarah, is in that area."
- **Action:** Select the request and assign it to Rider 'Sarah'.
"The request instantly moves from 'Open' to 'Assigned'. The Retailer's dashboard is also instantly updated in the background."

## 4. The Rider View & Gamification (1 minute)
*(Presenter 3 takes over, switches to Rider View)*
"I am Sarah, the Rider. Before I start, I use our seamless Profile Selector to log into my specific Rider Profile."
- **Action:** Click 'Sarah' on the Profile Selection Screen.
"I look at my dashboard and see I have a new assignment for a Blender to Westlands. Notice my current Rewards Points at the top."
- **Action:** Click 'Mark as Picked Up' on the assigned request.
"I've picked up the item. The status is updated for the Dispatcher. Now I arrive at the customer's location."
- **Action:** Fill in 'John Doe' as recipient and click 'Confirm Delivery (+50 pts)'.
"I hand over the package and collect the digital signature. Immediately, the item is marked Delivered, and you can see my Rewards Points instantly shoot up by 50 points! This gamification keeps our fleet highly motivated."

## 5. Conclusion (30 seconds)
*(Presenter 1)*
"In under two minutes, a request was logged, assigned, and delivered with complete transparency, accountability, and rider motivation. That is Reflex. We're happy to take your questions regarding our Monorepo architecture or trade-offs."
